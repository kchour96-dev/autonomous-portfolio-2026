Root Cause Analysis

The digital landscape is currently navigating a significant cybersecurity challenge, with Microsoft threat analysts detailing persistent cloud intrusions since May 2026. These advanced campaigns leverage sophisticated social engineering tactics, including voice phishing, SMS, and Microsoft Teams messages, to impersonate IT helpdesks. Attackers trick corporate employees into 'updating passkeys' or 'MFA settings,' ultimately surrendering critical session tokens and authentication approvals. Once access is gained, threat actors move swiftly to establish persistence by registering their own authentication methods, bypassing future MFA prompts and enabling extensive data exfiltration from cloud repositories, emails, and attachments.
