Market Impact

Further exacerbating the security narrative is the weaponization of Artificial Intelligence by scammers. Chainalysis’s 2026 Crypto Crime Report reveals that AI-enabled scam operations are a formidable force, proving 4.5 times more profitable per campaign, averaging $3.2 million compared to $719,000 for traditional methods.
