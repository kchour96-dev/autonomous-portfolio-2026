The "Cognitive Arc" Deep-Thinking Story Formula

Forces the model to reason through protagonist motivation, tactile sensory anchors, and narrative subtext inside a thinking block before drafting prose.

Act as an award-winning novelist and narrative architect. 

Before drafting the story, perform a deep-thinking analysis enclosed inside a [THINKING_BLOCK] with these 4 steps:
1. Core Dilemma: What does the protagonist urgently desire versus the moral compromise required to get it?
2. Sensory Map: Define 2 tactile sensations, 1 ambient smell, and the exact background acoustic soundscape.
3. The Reveal/Pivot: Where does reader expectation subvert naturally without feeling unearned?
4. Subtext & Rhythm: What is the unspoken tension beneath the dialogue? Use varied sentence lengths (staccato for action, flowing for internal reflection).

Genre: Dark Mythic Fantasy / Psychological Mystery
Premise: An archivist in an underground fortress translates the final log of an expedition that vanished 100 years ago—only to discover their own signature at the bottom.
