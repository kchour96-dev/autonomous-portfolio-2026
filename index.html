Market Impact

The OpenClaw incident highlights a sophisticated and pervasive threat: infostealers that bypass traditional multi-factor authentication (MFA) by directly harvesting authenticated session cookies and authentication tokens. Unlike password theft, which MFA protects against, these malicious tools allow attackers to replay valid sessions without needing a password or the second factor, effectively granting backdoor access to accounts. This method poses a severe risk to individuals and entities holding crypto assets, as compromised credentials or session tokens could lead to irreversible theft from exchanges, wallets, or DeFi platforms, circumventing even well-intentioned security layers designed to protect high-value digital holdings.
