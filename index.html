Market Impact

A critical cybersecurity threat has emerged in the form of 'PEEP', a sophisticated Chromium-based post-exploitation toolkit. PEEP masquerades as a benign browser extension, specifically targeting Chrome and Edge, to establish a persistent backdoor on compromised systems. This toolkit, built upon the open-source RedExt framework, significantly expands its capabilities to include dedicated installation routines, a native host bridge, heartbeat telemetry, and an update channel. It functions as a potent remote access and browser monitoring agent, adept at exfiltrating sensitive data like browsing history, active-tab metadata, and session cookies, while also enabling the execution of host commands and credential theft, all within the trusted browser process.
